🧠 Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between market sentiment (Fear/Greed Index) and trader behavior (profitability, volume, and volatility) using:
Bitcoin Market Sentiment Dataset
Hyperliquid Historical Trader Data
Objective: Identify how trading behavior aligns or diverges from market sentiment to extract insights useful for trading strategies.

⚙️ Methodology

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (timestamps, irrelevant columns removed).
Daily Aggregation:
Average & total PnL
PnL Volatility (std)
Average & total trade size
Merge with Sentiment Dataset.
Visualization of trends across Fear, Greed, and Neutral sentiment days.

📊 Key Findings

Greed Days: Larger average trade sizes and higher trading volumes.
Fear Days: Lower participation but higher volatility in profits/losses.
Neutral Days: Most stable trading conditions.
